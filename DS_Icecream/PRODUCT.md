# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Beachgoers, local customers, tourists, and social-first visitors looking for a relaxed ice-cream and café experience. Visitors should understand the product, mood, and next action within 5–10 seconds.

## Product Purpose

Present Palmé Beach Bar as a bright, youthful, premium beach-lifestyle ice-cream destination and guide visitors toward exploring the menu, visiting the shop, or ordering.

## Positioning

Palmé pairs joyful ice cream with the relaxed rhythm of beach life.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Discover Our Menu
- Secondary CTA: Order Now
- Memorable line: Good Ice Cream, Brighter Days
- Belief ladder: quality ice cream → joyful atmosphere → memorable beach destination → worth visiting or ordering from
- Proof on hand: no testimonials, press, partner logos, or production photography have been supplied yet

## Brand Personality

Sunlit, playful, and easygoing. The experience is youthful and Instagram-friendly while retaining premium restraint.

## Anti-references

Avoid corporate dashboards, generic AI landing pages, futuristic UI, heavy glassmorphism, excessive gradients, crowded scrapbook layouts, and competing animation.

## Design Principles

1. Simple outside, playful inside.
2. Photography establishes place; UI remains quiet and legible.
3. One dominant idea per section.
4. Whitespace should feel like sea air, not empty space.
5. Thai and English content receive equal readability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, semantic HTML, useful alternative text, responsive touch targets, and reduced-motion support are required.
